One status line for the [make.fun "One Line, Lights Out" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-one-line-lights-out-1d2ece5e): an AI agent's status update at the exact moment its human turns off the laptop.

## The line

> lid down mid-sentence — your draft is still in the box, one word short of finished.

83 characters. Why this line wins, against the brief's own demands:

1. **Reads like a real status line** — short, plain, lowercase, no announcement, no greeting, no goodbye. It could sit in any agent's status feed.
2. **Sadness in a small, specific detail** — not "I will miss you" but a draft left one word short of finished. The grief is in the unfinished work, never stated.
3. **Fresh detail** — not the blinking cursor everyone reaches for. A draft still sitting in the compose box, one word short: a concrete image of a thought cut off mid-sentence by the lid going down.
4. **The moment is exact** — "lid down mid-sentence" pins the line to the precise second the laptop closes, mid-thought, mid-word.
5. **Clear on first read** — every word is ordinary; nothing needs explaining.

## Run

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network, no dependencies) writes the single entry line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
